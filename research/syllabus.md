# Latin (Λατινικά) Panhellenic exams: official syllabus and exam format

Research date: 2026-11-01. Target exam: **Wednesday 2 June 2027, 08:30, 3 hours** (Ο.Π. Ανθρωπιστικών Σπουδών, Γ' ΓΕΛ).
Everything marked **[READ]** I opened and read in full (PDF or page text). **[SNIPPET]** means only a search snippet or a secondary summary. **[INFERRED]** is my own deduction from documents I read.

---

## 1. The one-paragraph answer

The exam has **no unseen text**. The two (in 2022: three) passages to translate are **verbatim excerpts from the school textbook's lessons** (Πασχάλης-Σαββαντίδης, Λατινικά: Τεύχος Α΄ lessons XVI-XX, Τεύχος Β΄ lessons XXI-L, 35 texts in total). They are cut with "......" between excerpts. I checked the Latin of the 2022, 2024, 2025 and 2026 papers against the textbook text and it matches word for word (see section 5). The paper is always **100 marks, 4 sections (Α, Β, Γ, Δ)**: translation 20, "introduction" literature question 10, etymology 10, morphology 30, syntax 30. Grammar and syntax examined = everything taught in Β' Λυκείου (lessons I-XV) plus the Γ' list in section 3. No syllabus change between 2025-26 and 2026-27 (Latin section identical apart from the "ΒΙΒΛΙΑ 2026-2027" heading).

### 3.1 Β' Λυκείου material that is also examined ("η ύλη που έχει διδαχθεί στη Β' ΓΕΛ") [READ in guidelines]
Lessons I-XV of Τεύχος Α΄: 1st declension; 2nd declension (-us, neuter, -er); 3rd declension (consonant stems, vowel stems, neuter vowel stems), 4th and 5th declension; adjectives (3rd decl.); personal pronouns, ille, is-ea-id, hic, ipse, idem, qui-quae-quod; indicative of all active tenses of all four conjugations and sum; possum, fero, volo-vis; passive present indicative and infinitive (1st, 2nd, 4th conj.), deponents; principal parts (αρχικοί χρόνοι); present/perfect infinitives; τελικό και ειδικό απαρέμφατο; απαρέμφατο με iubeo, veto, prohibeo, sino, patior, cogo; iterative cum; inverse cum; ποιητικό αίτιο; γενική και αφαιρετική ιδιότητας; capio-verbs; χρήσεις πτώσεων. (Plus literature intro, see section 2.)

### 3.2 What is NOT in the official Γ' syllabus
- Unseen/άγνωστο text (it exists only in the Ancient Greek syllabus, "ΑΔΙΔΑΚΤΟ ΚΕΙΜΕΝΟ" on p. 6 of the decision, not under Λατινικά).
- Post-classical literature (the intro stops at the Augustan age).
- Latin phrases used in Modern Greek (e.g. carpe diem, status quo, sine qua non, 41 items): these are listed as Β' τάξη examinable material in the Τράπεζα Θεμάτων document (pp. 10-11) and the Β' guidelines. Whether they can appear in Γ' Panhellenic via "η ύλη της Β'" is **uncertain**; none of the five papers asked about them. Good low-stakes bonus content.

## 4. Exam format (derived from the five official papers 2022-2026, all [READ])

Time 3 h; earliest leaving 10:00; blue/black pen; "Κάθε απάντηση τεκμηριωμένη είναι αποδεκτή".

| Θέμα | Marks | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Α1** | **20** | Translate two cut-and-pasted textbook excerpts (about 5-8 lines each; in 2022 three excerpts) |
| **Β1** | **10** | "Εισαγωγή" literature question, **closed type**: 2022 matching 5 authors to genres (2 spare); 2023 Σωστό/Λάθος x5; 2024 multiple choice (α/β/γ) x5; 2025 Σ/Λ x5; 2026 multiple choice x5 |
| **Β2** | **10** | Etymology, 5 items. Two styles: (i) **match** Latin words from the text to Greek words, 7 options with 2 spare (2022, 2025, 2026); (ii) **find in the passages** a cognate Latin word for each given Modern Greek word (2023: μετάδοση, ναυάγιο, νυχτερινός, απόσταση, μητρότητα; 2024: ρεαλιστής, σοσιαλισμός, στραγγαλίζω, φίρμα, ρήγας) |
| **Γ1** | **15** | ~11-13 noun/adjective/pronoun/adverb forms taken from the text, each with an instruction ("γενική στον άλλο αριθμό", "τους άλλους δύο βαθμούς") |
| **Γ2** | **15** | Verb forms. Since 2023 split in Γ2α (4 or 6 marks: find and transform items in a passage, e.g. participles, deponents, subjunctives) and Γ2β (9 or 11 marks: ~9-10 verb forms with instructions) |
| **Δ1** | **15** | Δ1α (10-12 marks): syntactic identification of 5-6 words ("είναι ……… στο ………"); Δ1β (3-5 marks): clause identification or a conversion |
| **Δ2** | **15** | Two conversion/analysis tasks (see table below) |
| Total | **100** | |

Marking scale: raw 100 per subject. (Exam score and weights per department not researched: see "uncertain" below.)

### 4.1 What each paper asked in Δ (syntax), the most unpredictable section

| Year | Δ1β | Δ2 |
|---|---|---|
| 2022 | Δευτερεύουσα (ne + abl. abs.): είδος, αιτιολόγηση εκφοράς, λειτουργία (5) | Δ2α turn two participles into subordinate clauses "με όλους τους δυνατούς τρόπους" (12); Δ2β indirect to direct speech (3) |
| 2023 | Active to passive (5) | Δ2α find two subordinate clauses, kind, function, justify mood (10); Δ2β direct to indirect speech after "Caecilia dixit" (5) |
| 2024 | Clause "qui non fateatur": είδος, λειτουργία, δικαιολόγηση εκφοράς (5) | Δ2α passive to active (7); Δ2β identify "Marco Tullio ... consulibus" (abl. abs.) and convert to a historical **cum** clause (8) |
| 2025 | Conditional: identify (εκφορά+είδος) and convert to possible-present-future (5) | Δ2α indirect to direct, 3 sentences (6); Δ2β active to passive (9) |
| 2026 | Place expressions: replace "in Africa" with Roma, Athenae, Carthago (3) | Δ2α identify cum clause (είδος, λειτουργία, τρόπος εισαγωγής, αιτιολόγηση εκφοράς) and contract to a participle (7); Δ2β express purpose by **ut-clause, supine, gerundive "ad + γερουνδιακό"** (8) |

### 4.2 Tally of question types, 5 papers read (2022-2026) [INFERRED counts from the papers]
- Γ1: every year includes (a) 3rd-declension nouns, (b) comparison of an adjective or adverb ("τους άλλους δύο βαθμούς"), (c) a pronoun form (ille/is/idem/quidam/ipse/hic), (d) a vocative or ablative or genitive plural. 5 of 5 years.
- Γ2: **gerund form AND supine form every year** (5/5: supine accusative 2022, 2026; supine ablative 2023, 2024, 2025; gerund gen/dat/abl across years). **Imperative** form every year (5/5; the future imperative appeared 2022 and 2026). **Subjunctive** forms every year (5/5; including pluperfect and perfect). Participles in 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026 (and 2022 via uterētur). Periphrastic conjugation explicitly in 2023 and 2025. Deponents explicit in 2025, implicit elsewhere.
- Δ1α: syntactic identification, every year (5/5).
- Subordinate-clause identification with justification of mood: every year (5/5).
- Direct/indirect speech conversion: 3 of 5 (2022, 2023, 2025). Active/passive conversion: 3 of 5 (2023, 2024, 2025). OEFE said in 2025 that indirect-to-direct "had not been asked for 4-5 years" and in 2026 that contraction of a clause into a participle and the expression of purpose had not been asked for 4-5 years; the first claim conflicts with the 2022 paper I read (3 marks for indirect-to-direct), so treat those OEFE remarks as loose.

## 5. Evidence that the exam texts are verbatim textbook texts [READ]

- 2026 paper, "Atilius Rēgulus, cum Poenos in Africā funderet ..." matches lesson **XXXV** (Seneca, Ad Helviam) in Τεύχος Β΄ word for word; "Porcia, Bruti uxor ..." matches lesson **XLIX**. OEFE: "δόθηκαν αποσπάσματα από τα κείμενα 35 και 49" https://www.alfavita.gr/panellinies/518656_latinika-2026-oi-kala-proetoimasmenoi-mathites-tha-aristeysoyn
- 2025 "Accipe nunc quid postea Nasīca fecerit ..." = lesson **XXIV** (Nasica and Ennius); "Sapientem nec paupertas nec dolor prohibet ..." = lesson **XXXIX**. OEFE: texts 24 and 39 https://www.alfavita.gr/panellinies/480013_panelladikes-2025-ektimiseis-tis-oiefe-gia-ta-latinika-poia-erotisi-ehei-na
- 2024 "Marco Tullio Cicerōne et Gaio Antōnio consulibus ..." = lesson **XIX** in Τεύχος Α΄; "qui spem Catilīnae mollibus sententiis aluērunt ..." = lesson **XLII**. Διακρότημα: texts 19 and 42 https://alfavita.gr/panellinies/448957_panellinies-2024-oi-ektimiseis-gia-mathima-ton-latinikon
- 2023: texts 23 and 38 https://www.alfavita.gr/panellinies/418558_panelladikes-2023-pos-ektimoyn-oi-ekpaideytikoi-bathmo-dyskolias-ton-thematon (Arria/Paetus and Caecilia; both present in the textbook).
- 2022: texts 16, 36, 45 https://www.alfavita.gr/panellinies/383421_panelladikes-2022-i-ektimisi-gia-ta-themata-ton-latinikon ("Nostri, postquam pila in hostes misērunt ..." = lesson XVI, text verified).
- The Β2 etymology items (e.g. 2025 domi-δωμάτιο, cognosco-απόγνωση, imperitos-πείρα, agunt-αγωγή, premi-πρέσα) correspond to the textbook's own "Ετυμολογικά" footnotes (e.g. lesson XXIV note: "< os = στόμα ... co-gnosco· πρβ. γι-γνώσκω").

Consequence: **the translation question rewards knowing the 35 prescribed texts** (vocabulary lists "ΛΕΞΙΛΟΓΙΟ" and notes are printed beside each text) and the grammar/etymology questions are built from words in those same texts.

## 7. Etymology (Β2) and the official etymology list [READ]

The 2025-26 and 2026-27 guidelines contain an official "ενδεικτικές ετυμολογικές συσχετίσεις" list per lesson 16-50 (Latin word, meaning, Modern Greek derivative, e.g. cado - κάζο, mercenarius - μερκαντιλισμός, natura - νατουραλισμός, res - ρεαλισμός, fortuna - φουρτούνα, mitto - κομισάριος/μισιονάριος, curro - κούρσα/κούριερ, servus - σερβιτόρος, vinco - Βίκτωρ). Items with * are "evidently Latin-derived" extras. File: `research/source-data/official-etymology-list-lessons-16-50.txt`. The guidelines say explicitly the list contains only Greek words that came **from Latin, usually via Romance languages**, not inherited cognates (pater/πατήρ). But the papers ask **both kinds**: Latin loans (2024: res-ρεαλιστής, socius-σοσιαλισμός, strangulo-στραγγαλίζω, confirmo-φίρμα, regie-ρήγας; a frontistirio forecast for 2026 used natura-νατουραλισμός and casus-κάζο, which are in the official list) and genuine cognates (2022: fugiunt-πρόσφυγας, magna-μεγαλοπρεπής, cernitur-διάκριση, cognoscantur-άγνωστος; 2025: domi-δωμάτιο, cognosco-απόγνωση, imperitos-πείρα, agunt-αγωγή). The textbook's "Ετυμολογικά" footnotes under each lesson are the real source of both.

It does **not** affect June 2027. Source: https://en.protothema.gr/2026/02/03/national-baccalaureate-panhellenic-exams-are-not-abolished-implementation-begins-in-the-2027-2028-school-year/ **[READ]**.

## 10. Implications for the app (for the other agents)

1. Core content = the **35 textbook texts XVI-L**: Latin text, accurate Greek translation, vocabulary (ΛΕΞΙΛΟΓΙΟ), notes, "Ετυμολογικά". Translation practice should be sentence-chunk based (the exam excerpts are 5-8 lines).
2. Grammar engine = the Γ' list in section 3 plus Β' basics, with **forms drilling** (Γ1/Γ2 style prompts, in the exact Greek exam wording: "τη γενική πτώση στον άλλο αριθμό", "το β΄ ενικό πρόσωπο της προστακτικής του μέλλοντα στην ίδια φωνή") and **syntax conversions** (active/passive, direct/indirect, participle/clause, purpose, conditionals, cum clauses, ablative absolute).
3. Always-tested forms (5/5 papers): comparison of adjectives and adverbs, gerund and supine forms, imperatives, subjunctives, participles, 3rd-declension nouns, pronouns.
4. Literature intro (Β1) is closed-type: Σ/Λ, matching, multiple choice. Build small "who wrote what" and "which period/genre" games from the syllabus intro only (stop at Augustan age).
5. Etymology (Β2): Latin word to Modern Greek word matching with distractors (exactly the paper's 5 plus 2 spare format).
6. Raw material for other agents is in `research/source-data/` (the three `textbook-*.txt` files are copyrighted state textbooks, git-ignored via `.gitignore`; use for reading and checking only, never publish them).
