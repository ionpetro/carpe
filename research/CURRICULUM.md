# Carpe: curriculum design (Λατινικά, Πανελλαδικές 2 Ιουνίου 2027)

Written 2026-11-01 by the lead-designer agent from the research in this folder (`syllabus.md`, `pastpapers.md`, `vocab.md` + `vocab.jsonl`, `morphology.md` + `morphology-past-items.json`, `syntax.md`, `texts.md`, `traps.md`, `derivatives.md` + `hooks.jsonl`, the official bank files). Nothing below is from memory: every exam fact points at one of those files, and every Latin form a writer puts into a unit must be checked against the school books (`source-data/textbook-*.txt`, `source-data/textbook-tzartzanos-latin-grammar.txt`) before it ships.

Audience: one Greek 17-year-old in Γ΄ Λυκείου (Ομάδα Προσανατολισμού Ανθρωπιστικών Σπουδών) who has done Β΄ Λυκείου Latin (lessons I-XV) and now studies lessons XVI-L at school. UI language: modern Greek, school terminology (Τζάρτζανος / Πασχάλης-Σαββαντίδης). Tone: warm, dry, never childish. Mascot: Remo (wolf cub, laurel wreath). Brand line: «Carpe diem. Carpe το 20.»

## 1. Course map (13 units, path order)

Each unit is one file `app/src/units/uN.js`, written by one agent. Lessons are 3-4 minutes (7-10 graded steps). Vocabulary/text units are bigger and their lessons may hold 25-35 items; the engine then serves a lesson in two or three sittings («συνέχεια») instead of one.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| u1 | Μιλάς ήδη Λατινικά (ρίζες, δάνεια, Θέμα Β2) | Β2 | 7 | 180 hooks/pairs |
| u2 | Κείμενα & λεξιλόγιο Ι: μαθήματα 16-27 | Α1, Β2, Γ, Δ | 6 (two textbook lessons each) | ~170 words + ~60 sentences |
| u3 | Ονόματα: κλίσεις, γενική πληθυντικού, κλητική, τα παράξενα | Γ1 | 7 | ~120 form cards |

| u5 | Κείμενα & λεξιλόγιο ΙΙ: μαθήματα 28-39 | Α1, Β2, Γ, Δ | 6 | ~130 words + ~60 sentences |
| u6 | Επίθετα, παραθετικά, επιρρήματα, αντωνυμίες | Γ1 | 7 | ~130 cards |
| u7 | Ρήμα ΙΙ: απαρέμφατα, μετοχές, γερούνδιο, γερουνδιακό, σουπίνο, περιφραστική, ανώμαλα | Γ2 | 8 | ~180 cards |

**Path and unlocking.** Skepsis unlocks lessons strictly in sequence. Carpe keeps sequential unlocking **inside** a unit but opens the **first lesson of every unit from day one**, so the student can follow what her school is doing this week (texts in u2/u5/u9, grammar in u3-u7, syntax in u8-u11) and still do the literature (u12) in a free evening. The path screen shows the units as 13 milestones on a Roman road (Via Appia); the roundup node at the end of each unit is «Ανακεφαλαίωση».

**Why this order.** It follows the textbook (lessons 16-27 introduce passive, subjunctive, imperative; 28-39 cases, gerund/supine, first clauses; 40-50 the remaining clauses, indirect speech, purpose). Grammar units sit next to the texts that introduce them. Syntax comes after forms, because every Δ task presumes Γ forms. Traps last, because the trap cards reference everything.

## 2. Content item kinds (the schema writers fill)

All units share one file shape:

```js
// app/src/units/u3.js
CARPE.registerUnit({
  id: "u3",
  title: "Ονόματα",
  blurb: "Η γενική πληθυντικού κρίνει το Θέμα Γ1.",
  exam: ["Γ1"],                 // which exam sections this unit feeds (for Θέμα της ημέρας and mocks)
  lessons: [
    { id: "u3l1", title: "Διάβασε την εκφώνηση", items: [ /* items */ ] },
  ],
});
```

Every item has `id` (unique across the whole course, prefix with the unit, e.g. `u3-urbs-genpl`), `kind`, `say` (one-breath Greek teaching line, ≤ 160 characters), `status` (`"book"` = checked in the school textbook or Τζάρτζανος, `"exam"` = taken from a real paper/answer, `"general"` = standard Latin not in the school books, `"unsure"` = flag for the human reviewer) and `src` (free text: `"XXIV Π6"`, `"Παν. 2026 Γ1"`, `"Τζάρτζανος §26"`). Optional `lesson` (16-50) ties the item to a textbook lesson for the text units' «από το μάθημα» filter.

| kind | Fields | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `word` | `la` (lemma as printed, macrons kept), `forms` (dictionary form: nouns `lemma, gen.`; adjectives all genders; verbs 4 principal parts, deponents `lemma, -us sum, inf.`), `pos` (noun/verb/adj/adv/pron/prep/conj/num/phrase), `gender` (m/f/n/c or null), `decl` (nouns 1-5, adjectives `2-1-2` or `3`, verbs `1`/`2`/`3`/`3-io`/`4`/`irreg`, plus `dep`/`semidep` flags), `el` (book meaning, senses `;`-separated), `hook` (memory hook in Greek: derivative, cognate, phrase, pop fact; from `hooks.jsonl`), `etym` (optional list of Greek derivatives for Β2, each `{el, official: true/false}`), `trap` (optional: the form fact that costs marks, e.g. «γεν. πληθ. dotium»), `lessons` (where the book glosses it) | u2, u5, u9 |
| `sentence` | `la` (as printed, with macrons and the book's typos), `el` (our own Greek translation, exam register), `chunks` (ordered Greek tiles, 4-8), `gloss` (list of `{la, el}` for 2-4 hard words), `notes` (list of `{w, role_el, dep}` for 1-3 syntax facts in the sentence, e.g. `{w:"consulibus", role_el:"ιδιόμορφη απόλυτη αφαιρετική (χρόνος)", dep:"coniuravit"}`), `exam` (years this sentence was in a paper, from `pastpapers.md` §3, else `[]`) | u2, u5, u9 |
| `form` | `lemma`, `given` (the form as it stands in a text, e.g. `dotem`), `ctx` (short Latin context, optional), `ask` (exact exam-style Greek instruction, e.g. «τη γενική πτώση, στον άλλο αριθμό»), `cell` (machine key `gen.pl`, `impv.fut.2sg.act`, `sup.acc`, `ger.gen`, `ptc.fut.nom.sg.f` ...), `answer` (list of accepted strings, no macrons, first = preferred), `distractors` (3-5 wrong forms from neighbouring cells of the same lemma; the engine may add more via `morph.js`), `tiles` (optional stem+sign+ending split for the build game, e.g. `["dot","i","um"]`), `rule` (the one-breath rule), `why` (2-line explanation shown after answering) | u3, u4, u6, u7, u13 |
| `rule` | `title`, `say`, `ex` (list of `{la, el}` 2-4 examples), `terms` (school terms this rule introduces), `quiz` (list of 2-5 inline questions: `{t:"mcq", q, options, a, why}` or `{t:"tf", q, a, why}` or `{t:"bucket", buckets:[...], items:[{text, bucket}]}`) | all grammar/syntax units |
| `role` | Δ1α item: `la` (sentence), `w` (the word asked), `answer` (list; first is the marker phrase, e.g. «απρόθετη αφαιρετική του μέτρου ή της διαφοράς»), `dep` (the word it depends on, e.g. «στο post»), `tiles` (phrase split for building: `["απρόθετη αφαιρετική","του μέτρου ή της διαφοράς","στο post"]`), `distractors` (3 plausible wrong roles), `why` | u8, u13 |
| `clause` | Δ1β/Δ2α item: `la` (sentence with the clause marked by « »), `kind_el` (είδος), `fn_el` (συντακτική λειτουργία), `intro_el` (τρόπος εισαγωγής), `mood_el` (έγκλιση + reason), `tense_el` (χρόνος + reason, incl. ακολουθία / ιδιομορφία), `distractors` per part (lists), `contract` (optional: participle/abl. abs. equivalent), `expand` (optional list of clause rewrites) | u10, u11 |
| `transform` | Δ2 item: `from` (Latin), `ask` (Greek instruction as the exam words it), `to` (list of accepted Latin answers; first = model), `tiles` (word tiles incl. 2-4 distractor forms), `orderFree` (true when Latin word order may vary), `changed` (count of words that change, for feedback), `steps` (ordered list of 3-6 Greek micro-steps for the faded worked example), `why` | u11, u10 |
| `fact` | Β1 item: `q` (statement or question), `t` (`"tf"` or `"mcq"`), `a` (boolean or correct option), `options` (for mcq), `why` (one line citing the intro), `bankId` (the number in the ministry bank file, or `"textbook"`) | u12 |
| `hook` | Β2/etymology item: `la` (Latin word), `el` (Greek relative), `type` (`"cognate"` / `"loan"` / `"official"` / `"phrase"` / `"calque"`), `lessons`, `story` (one line why), `source` | u1 |
| `origin` | «Λατινικό ή όχι;» item: `w` (Greek word), `latin` (boolean), `from` (one line) | u1, roundups |
| `trap` | `wrong` (a plausible wrong answer), `right` (the right one), `ask` (the instruction that produced it), `why`, `cat` (`"read"`, `"noun"`, `"verb"`, `"syntax"`, `"marking"`) | u13, plus 1-2 in each grammar unit |

Answer normalisation (engine): lowercase, strip macrons/breves, collapse spaces, treat `v`/`u` and `i`/`j` as printed in the book (we never ask the student to type macrons; we show them on the question word). Accepted variants live in `answer`/`to` lists: `iis/eis/is`, `deis/diis/dis`, `sci(v)isse`, `amavissemus/amassemus`, `Carthagine/Carthagini`.

## 3. Question types the engine must support

Skepsis has `intro`, `mcq`, `build`, `match`, `tf`. Carpe keeps all five and adds the ones marked NEW. Every type still returns `{type, itemId, label, prompt, ..., answer, explain}` so `app.js` can render it and `engine.grade` can score it.

| # | Type | Skepsis trick kept | Latin use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `mcq` | guess-first opener («Μάντεψε πρώτα», no penalty), 4 options, distractors from the same unit | meaning of a word; which case/number a form is; pick the Greek term; decode the instruction («τη δοτική στον άλλο αριθμό» → which cell?) |
| 2 | `cell` NEW (mcq variant) | distractors from **neighbouring cells of the same lemma** (other number, other case, other tense) | Γ1/Γ2 «pick the right form»: `dotem` → gen. pl.: dotium / dotum / dotibus / dotis |
| 3 | `type` NEW | typed answer with the normaliser above; «Δείξε μου πλακίδια» button downgrades to `build` without penalty the first time | the real exam experience for Γ1/Γ2 forms and short Δ2 answers (one word) |
| 4 | `build` | tiles in order; extra distractor tiles | stem + tense sign + ending (`leg-eba-m`, `dot-i-um`, `audi-end-i`); Greek translation chunks of a sentence; the marker phrase «X είναι [όρος] [στο λέξη]» |
| 5 | `slots` NEW | labelled slots filled from a tile pool | **αρχικοί χρόνοι** builder: 4 slots (ενεστώτας, παρακείμενος, σουπίνο, απαρέμφατο) from 8 tiles, distractors from look-alike verbs (cado/caedo, lego/legi vs lexi) |
| 6 | `match` (+ `spare`) | pairs | Β2 exam format: 5 Latin words left, **7** Greek words right, 2 spare; also nominative↔genitive, verb↔principal parts, Greek term↔Latin construction, direct↔indirect form |
| 7 | `tf` | yes/no sorting, fast | Σ/Λ (Β1); «Λατινικό ή όχι;»; «-ium ή -um;»; «αποθετικό;»; «οριστική ή υποτακτική;» |
| 8 | `bucket` NEW | 2-4 labelled buckets, swipe/tap | sort clauses into τελική / συμπερασματική / βουλητική / ενδοιαστική; sort conditionals into α΄/β΄/γ΄ είδος; sort cum uses; sort ablatives by group |
| 9 | `find` NEW | tap words in a Latin sentence (multi-select) | Γ2α «εντοπίστε τις μετοχές / τα αποθετικά / τις υποτακτικές»; Β2 «εντοπίστε στο κείμενο τη συγγενή λέξη»; Δ «βρες τη δευτερεύουσα» |
| 10 | `steps` NEW | a 4-step chain of `mcq`s whose chosen answers accumulate into one paragraph; scored per step like the exam (1+1+1+2) | the clause justification (είδος → λειτουργία → εισαγωγή → εκφορά: έγκλιση + χρόνος + ακολουθία) |
| 11 | `transform` NEW (build variant) | all correct tiles must be chosen; `orderFree` allows any order; wrong-form tiles as distractors; shows «άλλαξαν N λέξεις» | active↔passive, direct↔indirect, participle↔clause, purpose in 3 ways, place names, prohibition |
| 12 | `spot` NEW | tf + follow-up mcq | spot-the-trap: a student answer is shown, decide right/wrong, then pick the fix |
| 13 | `swipe` NEW | self-graded flashcard (flip, «Το ξέρω» / «Όχι ακόμη»), 3 s per card | fast vocabulary review of 20-30 words; counts as a review grade (self-report) with half weight |
| 14 | `decode` | «decode an unseen item» (transfer) | apply a rule to a lemma never shown: «Με τον κανόνα -ium: γενική πληθυντικού του *mens, mentis*?»; a fresh sentence from a text not yet in a paper: «οριστική ή υποτακτική, και γιατί;» |
| 15 | `intro` | teach card | word card (lemma, forms, gender, meaning, hook, trap), rule card (say + 2 examples), sentence card (Latin, gloss, our translation) |

Speed round: **«Κολοσσαίο»** = Skepsis rodeo: 3-option questions, 20 s each, one miss ends the run, score = gladiators defeated. Pools: `form` cards (Γ1/Γ2 style), `role` cards (Δ1α), `hook` cards, `fact` cards. Best score badge. Pulls only from items the student has learned (box ≥ 1) or from u1/u3 if new.

Missed questions return at the end of the lesson (unchanged from Skepsis). Confetti on perfect lesson, Remo cheers; Remo looks sad only in the explanation panel, never blocks progress.

## 4. Game features (Roman flavour, same mechanics as Skepsis)

- **XP = δηνάρια.** 10 per lesson, bonus 5 for a perfect lesson, 3 per Κολοσσαίο kill, 15 for a mock section.
- **Daily goal** tiers rename Skepsis's Quick/Steady/Hoplite: **Tiro** (10, 1 lesson, ~3 min), **Miles** (20), **Centurio** (40). A «Ελάχιστη μέρα» of 5 minutes keeps the streak: one Θέμα της ημέρας or one review set.
- **Streak** («σερί»): weekends never break it; one free freeze («άδεια») a week, +1 every 7 days, max 2. No hearts, no lives, no punishment. Remo holds a shield when a freeze was used.
- **Badges = Roman ranks and honours**: Tiro (first lesson), Miles (7-day streak), Decurio (12 lessons), Centurio (30-day streak), Tribunus (all form cards of u3+u4 in box ≥ 3), Legatus (all 35 text lessons done), Triumphator (Κολοσσαίο 15+), Pontifex (u12 complete, Β1 mock 10/10), Augur (10 Θέματα της ημέρας in a row), Carpe το 20 (a full mock at 20/20 scaled), Flawless → «Sine errore».
- **Remo moods**: happy (default), cheer (perfect / badge / streak milestone), sad (after a miss, with the one-breath fix), sleepy (countdown screen at night), gladiator helmet (Κολοσσαίο).
- **Countdown**: header chip «212 μέρες ως την Τετάρτη 2 Ιουνίου» with three phases shown as a road: Νοέμβριος-Ιανουάριος «Θεμέλια» (learn units), Φεβρουάριος-Απρίλιος «Οχύρωση» (weekly mock, review), Μάιος «Via Appia» (review only, daily Θέμα, box-6 cards pulled forward). The date is stored in one constant and flagged «επιβεβαίωσέ την τον Μάρτιο».
- **Θέμα της ημέρας** (daily exam-style drill, 2-4 minutes, seeded by the date so it is the same if she reopens): Δευτέρα Γ1 (4 `form` items, nominal), Τρίτη Γ2 (1 `find` + 3 verb forms), Τετάρτη Δ1 (3 `role` + 1 `clause` via `steps`), Πέμπτη Δ2 (1 `transform`), Παρασκευή Β1+Β2 (3 `fact` + 1 `match` 5+2), Σάββατο Α1 (2 `sentence` builds), Κυριακή mixed from the weakest boxes. Scored with the exam's marks for that section and shown as «σήμερα: 12/15». Draws only from units she has started, else from the first lesson of the relevant unit (so it also teaches).
- **Προσομοίωση** (mock): «Μικρό διαγώνισμα» 20 minutes = one paper scaled: Α1 2 sentences (20), Β1 5 facts (10), Β2 one match 5+2 (10), Γ1 6 forms (15), Γ2 one find + 4 forms (15), Δ1 4 roles + 1 clause (15), Δ2 1 transform (15). Marks per component like the paper; result screen shows the 7 bars and what to review. «Μεγάλο διαγώνισμα» = a real past paper (2022-2026) as a 3-hour timer with the real wording, answers self-checked against our key (the papers are ministry documents; our keys are our own, cross-checked with the Διακρότημα/ΟΕΦΕ keys in `pastpapers-raw/`). Unlocks after u3+u4+u8 first lessons.
- **Τετράδιο λαθών** (mistake notebook): every item missed twice sits here with its `why`; the review session takes 30% from it. Cearsplus tip, `traps.md` §6.
- **Stress design**: optional 2-minute «γράψε την ανησυχία» card in the last week (Ramirez & Beilock, labelled as a tip), one line «η καρδιά που χτυπάει γρήγορα ετοιμάζεται, δεν φοβάται» (Jamieson 2010) on the exam-eve screen. Never a league with strangers; an opt-in «δύο αδέλφια» board (she and the user) with a shared weekly goal before any ranking.

## 5. Spaced review and how it covers the vocabulary

Leitner boxes as in Skepsis: 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days, plus a **6th box at 60 days** (Cepeda et al. 2008: gaps grow with the retention interval; 7 months out, days then weeks). A miss drops to box 1. From 1 May 2027 the engine caps every `due` at `min(due, 2027-05-26)` so every card gets one last look before the exam.

Item keys:
- `word` → `u2-dos` (one card per lemma; the review question rotates: meaning, reverse, genitive/gender pick, principal parts slots, hook match).
- `form` → `(lemma, cell)` as in `morphology.md` §10.2, e.g. `dos|gen.pl`, `fero|impv.fut.2sg`.
- `role`, `clause`, `transform`, `fact`, `hook`, `trap` → their own ids.

Load estimate: ~530 core words + ~150 non-core text words, ~600 form cards, ~120 roles, ~100 clauses, ~90 transforms, ~120 facts, ~180 hooks, ~90 traps ≈ 2,000 cards. Each card is seen ~6 times before box 6 → ~12,000 graded answers over 210 days ≈ 55 a day. A lesson gives 8-10, a review set 10, Θέμα της ημέρας 4-6, a `swipe` vocabulary set 25 in two minutes, a Κολοσσαίο run 8-15. So a 15-20 minute day covers it; a 5-minute day keeps the streak and the most urgent cards. The practice builder orders: due items first (oldest due first, lowest box first), then Τετράδιο λαθών, then weakest boxes, and it **interleaves kinds** (never five forms of the same lemma in a row; Pan et al. 2019: interleaving helps across sessions).

Vocabulary specifically: the 532 `core: true` entries of `vocab.jsonl` are all taught in u2/u5/u9 (by first examinable lesson). Tier-1 non-core words appear only as `gloss` on sentences and as distractors; they are learnable on tap («πρόσθεσέ τη στο λεξιλόγιό μου») which creates a card. The `swipe` set each day is 25 words: 15 due + 10 from the lesson her school is on (she sets «στο σχολείο είμαστε στο μάθημα 31» in settings; default follows the path).

## 6. The morphology engine (`morph.js`) and accuracy rules

- Build a small pure-JS generator: lemma + class + principal parts + exceptions table → any cell. Regression tests: `research/morphology-past-items.json` (143 real items, with the key corrections listed in `morphology.md` §1.5) and the paradigm tables of Τζάρτζανος (§§14-103).
- Hand-written `form` items in the unit files always carry their `answer` explicitly; `morph.js` is used to **cross-check** them in `test/` and to generate neighbour-cell distractors and endless Κολοσσαίο items. If generator and hand answer disagree, the test fails and a human decides.
- Writers never invent a form, meaning or gender. Sources in order of authority: the lesson ΛΕΞΙΛΟΓΙΟ and glossary (`vocab.jsonl` is parsed from them), Τζάρτζανος, the textbook ΠΑΡΑΤΗΡΗΣΕΙΣ, then a real paper's key (tutor keys, flagged). Anything else is `status: "general"` or `"unsure"`.
- «Seen in exam» labels come only from `morphology-past-items.json`, `passages-by-paper.json`, `pastpapers.md` or `syntax.md` §1.2, with the year.
- No statistics in the UI except the mark scheme and the tallies in this file's §0, phrased as «στα 5 διαγωνίσματα 2022-2026 ζητήθηκε…», never as a probability.
- Macrons: shown on question words and in texts as the book prints them; never required in answers.
- Copyright: textbook texts are © ΙΤΥΕ. The app quotes the 3-6 key sentences per lesson that `texts.md` already quotes (short fragments) plus the sentences that appear in the public exam papers. Full lesson texts are not shipped unless the owner gets permission (then `research/private/latin_full.json`, regenerated by `research/tools/extract_textbook_latin.py`, is the source). Greek translations are our own.

## 7. Unit briefs

Each brief below is what a writer agent receives (plus §2's schema). «Read» lists the files to open. Counts are targets, not quotas; accuracy beats volume.

### u1 · Μιλάς ήδη Λατινικά (ρίζες, δάνεια, Θέμα Β2)

Focus: the 10 marks of Β2 and the hooks that make the rest stick. ### u13 · Παγίδες, εκφώνηση, βαθμολογητές, η μέρα της εξέτασης

Focus: the marks lost to reading, not to ignorance, and the marking conventions. Everything here is a `trap` or `spot` card that references other units; the unit is also the deck the Τετράδιο λαθών feeds.

Read: `traps.md` §3, §4.1-4.5, §8; `morphology.md` §1.5 (key errors), §7 (30 clusters), §8; `syntax.md` §8 (26 traps), §11; `pastpapers.md` §9; `texts.md` §3 (typos).

Lessons:
1. **Διάβασε την εκφώνηση, προχωρημένο** (`spot`, `mcq`): ίδιο/άλλο αριθμό, γένος, φωνή; «τους άλλους δύο» vs «τους τρεις βαθμούς» (the paper bolds δύο/τρεις); «επιρρήματος» attached to an adjective form (certissimum → certe; optimum → melius; multi → multum/plus/plurimum); two-answer items (2022 ille; 2023 petit gen. and abl.; 2023 fecit present and future imperative); «να ληφθεί υπόψη το υποκείμενο» (miraturos esse / miratos fore; fuerint ascensuri); missing voice (2026 venit: check what exists); macrons separate venit/vēnit, legit/lēgit, fugit/fūgit; «vis» verb or noun.
2. **Παγίδες μορφολογίας** (`spot`, `trap`): -ium vs -um (noctium but sororum, sedum), neuter plurals (pondera, rura), 4th-declension genitive -us (vultus), res/rei/re, deus forms, vis/vires, pronoun genitives (cuiusdam, eorundem, illius, earum), mihi ipsi → ego ipse, superlatives -rimus/-limus, adverb ladders, supine from the 3rd part (acceptu not accipitu), gerund case endings (veniendo not veniendi for dative), dic/duc/fac/fer vs confice, fite, conferto, latum iri vs latum esse, deponent has no other voice, passive vs deponent (vocatus vs elapso), periphrastic agreement, possum/volo/fero/eo/fio oddities, compounds (attuli, allatum; accido vs occido), the book's typos. ~30 cards, each with the wrong answer a hurried student writes.
3. **Παγίδες συντακτικού** (`spot`, `trap`): the 26 of `syntax.md` §8 (Carthago city vs region; «εμπρόθετη γενική γερουνδίου» means gerundive; no active perfect participle; accusative subject of ειδικό απαρέμφατο even in ταυτοπροσωπία; τελικό only present; present imperative never prohibits; ne vs ut non; final clauses never perfect; quod indicative vs subjunctive, cum causal always subjunctive; cum moods; dum moods; dative of agent only with gerundive; price genitive vs ablative; genitive of quality for class/age/measure; persons as instruments per/opera; suus/se vs eius; relative in indirect speech → subjunctive; quin/quominus/ne; fear ne vs ut; si quis not si aliquis; impersonal passive of dative verbs; no attraction with neuter pronoun; curari = υποκείμενο του απροσώπου placuit; amoris objective; se object vs subject).
4. **Οι βαθμολογητές** (`rule`, `tf`): «Κάθε απάντηση τεκμηριωμένη είναι αποδεκτή» means justify; write the word the function attaches to («στο …»); accepted variants (iis/eis/is; deis/diis/dis; sci(v)isse; Carthagine/Carthagini); ΚΕΕ 2025: the question mark in direct questions is required; only «Ego domi non sum» accepted; the prepositional agent later accepted for inanimate; marks are split by component (2026 Δ2α 1+1+1+2+2), so write every component even if unsure of one; keys differ between tutor groups, so use the textbook term. Also: what the paper looks like (4 pages, ΚΕΙΜΕΝΑ then Α1, ΠΑΡΑΤΗΡΗΣΕΙΣ Β1-Δ2, blue/black pen, earliest leaving 10:00).
5. **Η μέρα της εξέτασης** (`rule` cards + 5 `mcq`): a 3-hour plan by marks (translation 35 min, Β 15, Γ 45, Δ 50, check 35); the six-step translation routine (verb → subject → main vs subordinate → conjunction/participle/abl. abs. → cases → vocabulary); the identification routine for Δ1α; what to do when a word is unknown (the passages are textbook text, so recall the lesson); the night before (sleep; no new material); the two optional stress tips, labelled as tips with their sources («γράψε την ανησυχία για 2 λεπτά», «η καρδιά που χτυπάει ετοιμάζεται»); the Remo send-off «Carpe το 20.»
